# 📊 Business Insights 360 | Power BI Dashboard

</p>

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights, enabling decision-makers to monitor performance, identify trends, and make strategic business decisions.

This project simulates a real-world business reporting environment where different departments require customized dashboards while executives need a consolidated view of overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build a centralized reporting solution that enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyze revenue and profitability
- Evaluate customer and product performance
- Track supply chain efficiency
- Measure marketing effectiveness
- Support strategic decision-making through interactive dashboards
